Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

SmartCart ("the app", "we") helps you make shopping lists, keep bills and track a budget. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and where it goes. SmartCart does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

1. Google sign-in

You can make shopping lists and use the budget without an account. To add bills, you sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication (Google). We then receive your name, email address and a Google account ID. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep your bills in your own account, separate from other users. We never post anything to your Google account. You can log out at any time from the side menu.

2. Information the app uses

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Google account: name, email, account ID Sign you in and link your bills to you Firebase Authentication, and on your phone while signed in
Shopping lists, budget, reminders and settings Core app features On your phone only

3. Scanning, including Urdu and Arabic

  • On-device reading: the app reads text on your phone without sending the image anywhere: Google ML Kit for English, Roman Urdu and Hindi, and the open-source Tesseract OCR for Urdu and Arabic.
  • Gemini (online): for shopping lists, and for bills the phone could not read, the image may be sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic. Gemini sends back only the text it read. Google handles this data under its own terms, linked below. We do not keep these images on our servers.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: cloud backup of bills and Gemini scanning.
  • Microphone: voice input, only while you are using it.
  • Location: to tag a bill with the shop's area. This is optional. If you deny it, the app still works.
  • Notifications: shopping reminders you set yourself.

You can change or remove any permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

5. Third-party services

SmartCart uses these Google services, which process data under their own policies:

  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase AI Logic (Gemini)
  • Google ML Kit and Google Play services (scanning, location)
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Gemini API terms: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

6. Feedback emails

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a message that already includes your app version, Android version, phone model and language, to help us fix problems. You can see and edit all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing. Information is only processed by the services listed above so the app can work, or if the law requires it.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Firestore security rules allow each signed-in account to read and write only its own bills. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleting a bill in the app also deletes its cloud copy.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Logging out stops new bills from being added and backed up. Bills already on the phone stay there.
  • To delete your account and all cloud copies of your bills, contact us (section 12) from the email of your Google account and we will remove them.

10. Children

SmartCart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
